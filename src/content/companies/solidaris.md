At Solidaris, a health insurance fund in Flanders, I took on the design lead role that nobody held yet. I brought design together across the Dutch and French side of the organisation, documented it all in zeroheight, and designed the tools staff use every day: a workflow app that combines document scanning with task tracking, an app for social workers, and a customer zone we rebuilt from scratch.
